How to play

Tap left / right to move, FLAP to fly. Strike another rider from above to unhorse them. Don’t fall in the lava. Flap on the beat for a stronger lift. Last rider standing wins.

Playing over a call? One person hosts and screen-shares the arena. Everyone else joins on their phone.
